One report at a time, on purpose. Each file goes through the three checks and you see what was read before adding the next, so a wrong or unreadable file never slips in unseen. Batch upload is left out to keep the numbers accurate.

Your file stays on this computer, is marked your upload, and is never mixed into the official data. Fix the company or type, switch it off, or remove it at any time in your library.

Your library

Reports on this computer

Nothing is deleted when a newer report arrives — older reports are the track record. Switch a report off to leave it out of the numbers and the valuation without losing it.

Official reports come from the exchange's published files and passed the sum checks, so the file is locked: you can switch it off, and still check its values one by one in Check numbers, but not change its pages or details. Your uploads are where mistakes are more likely (an unfamiliar layout, a page missed, the wrong company), so everything about them can be fixed.
